Privacy notice

Last updated: 7 October 2026

In short: we only collect what we need to help you with your admin. We never sell your details. You can ask us to see, change or delete your details at any time.

Who we are

CleanAdmin Ltd ("CleanAdmin", "we", "us") runs clean-admin.com. We help self-employed drivers, couriers and small removals teams with their admin.

If you have any question about your details, email us at clients@clean-admin.com.

What we collect

When you contact us on our website, WhatsApp or email, we collect:

  • Your name and phone number
  • Which platforms you use
  • Anything you write or send to us in your messages

When you become a client, we may also collect what we need to do the work, for example:

  • Your email address and home or business address
  • Your driving licence and other ID documents
  • Vehicle details, insurance documents and photos
  • Details of your jobs, customers and invoices
  • Access to your platform accounts, if you choose to give it to us

Why we use your details

  • To reply to your message
  • To do the work you ask us to do, such as sign-ups, invoices and review appeals
  • To send you our bills and keep records of payments
  • To follow the law, for example tax and accounting rules

We use your details because you asked us to help you, because we have an agreement with you, or because the law says we must keep certain records.

We do not send you marketing messages unless you say yes first.

Who we share your details with

We never sell your details. We only share them when we need to:

  • Platforms you ask us to set up or manage. We only send them what is needed for your account.
  • Insurance companies, only if you ask us to help you look for insurance.
  • Services we use to run our business: Google Workspace (email, file storage and our list of enquiries), WhatsApp (messages), GitHub (hosts our website) and our payment provider. They may only use your details to provide their service to us.
  • Officials, if the law says we must.

Your account passwords

If you give us access to a platform account, we use it only to do the work you asked for. We never share your password. We use the safest way the platform allows. When you stop using our service, please change your password.

How long we keep your details

  • If you contacted us but did not become a client: up to 12 months.
  • ID documents, licences and photos: we delete them within 30 days after the job is done or after you leave us.
  • Bills and payment records: as long as tax law says we must keep them.
  • Everything else: while you are a client, then up to 12 months after you leave.

How we keep your details safe

Only CleanAdmin staff who need your details can see them. Our accounts are protected with strong passwords and 2-step login. We do not keep your documents on loose paper or shared computers.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you the details we have about you
  • Correct details that are wrong
  • Delete your details
  • Stop using your details for a reason you do not agree with
  • Give you a copy of your details to take somewhere else

Email clients@clean-admin.com or message us on WhatsApp. We will reply within one month. It is free.

If you are not happy with how we use your details, please tell us first so we can fix it. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Our website

Our website does not use cookies for advertising or tracking. Everything on the page, including the fonts, loads from our own website. If you use the contact form, your details are saved in our Google Workspace account and sent to our email.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top of this page.
