Questions

Tap a question to see the answer.

Do I keep my account?

Yes. The account is always in your name. We use it only to do the work you ask for. You can change your password and remove our access at any time.

What if a platform says no?

Each platform makes its own choice. We cannot control it. If they say no, we tell you the reason if they give one. Where the platform allows it, we help you fix the problem and try again at no extra cost.

How do I pay?

We send you a payment link on WhatsApp or by email. You can pay by card or bank transfer. You pay Start-Up once. Management is paid each month.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Management is month to month. Send us a message to cancel. We stop at the end of the month you have paid for. There is no fee to cancel.

Is my data safe?

Yes. We only ask for what we need to do your admin. We keep your details private and never sell them. You can ask us to delete your details at any time.
